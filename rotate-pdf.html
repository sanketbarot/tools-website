FAQ

1

Can I rotate individual pages?

Yes! Each page has its own rotate left (-90°) and rotate right (+90°) buttons. You can rotate each page independently.

2

Can I rotate multiple pages at once?

Yes! Use "All +90°" to rotate every page, or select specific pages using checkboxes and click "Selected +90°".

3

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.

4

Does rotation affect quality?

No! Rotation uses the PDF's native page rotation property. The content is not re-rendered — zero quality loss.
