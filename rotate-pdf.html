FAQ

Can I rotate individual pages?

Yes! Click on page thumbnails to select them, then apply rotation. You can also click the rotate button on individual pages.

Does rotation affect quality?

No! Rotation only changes the page orientation metadata. All content remains in original quality.

Is it secure?

100% secure. Everything runs in your browser. No files uploaded.
