Our Story

AI ToolCor started as a personal frustration. Every time we needed to edit a PDF or convert a file, we were forced to either pay for expensive software or upload sensitive documents to random websites. We knew there had to be a better way — so we built it.

Early 2025

The Idea Was Born

Frustrated with expensive PDF software and privacy-invasive websites, we had one idea — build free, private, browser-based PDF & AI tools for everyone.

Mid 2025

Development Begins

Started building and testing core PDF tools. Focused on speed, privacy, and making everything run 100% inside the browser — no servers, no uploads.

Late 2025 – Early 2026

Building & Refining

Expanded the tool library to 30+ features including Merge, Split, Compress, Convert, OCR, and Formatter tools. Every tool tested and polished for the best user experience.

2026 🚀

Official Launch — Free for Everyone

AI ToolCor officially launched to the public — completely free, no account required, no file uploads, no limits. Built for everyone, forever.
