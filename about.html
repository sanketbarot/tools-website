About PDFTools

We build free, fast, and private online PDF tools for everyone. No signup, no uploads, no limits — everything runs in your browser.

Our Mission

PDFTools was created with one simple goal: make professional PDF tools accessible to everyone for free. Whether you're a student, professional, or business owner — you deserve great tools without paying hundreds of dollars for software.

Every tool on PDFTools runs 100% in your web browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server. What you do with your documents is your business — not ours.

Privacy First

Files never leave your device. Zero server uploads. Your data stays yours.

Speed Matters
