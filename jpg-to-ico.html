Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ICO file?

ICO is the standard icon format used for website favicons and Windows desktop icons. Browsers use ICO files to show your site icon in tabs and bookmarks.

Which size should I choose?

32×32 is the most common recommended size for website favicons. Use 16×16 for small browser tab icons, or 64×64 for higher resolution displays. Choose All Sizes ZIP to get every size at once!

What is included in the ZIP pack?

The ZIP pack includes both .ico and .png files for all 5 standard sizes: 16×16, 32×32, 48×48, 64×64 and 128×128. That's 10 files total — perfect for any project!

Should I use transparent background?

Yes, transparent background is best for logos and symbols. It allows your icon to blend nicely with browser themes and operating system backgrounds.

How do I use the ICO on my website?

Upload the downloaded .ico file to your website root and add:
<link rel="icon" href="favicon.ico"> inside your HTML <head> section.
