FAQ

JPG or PNG - which is better?

JPG = smaller files, good for photos. PNG = lossless quality, good for text/diagrams. WEBP = modern balance of both.

What DPI should I choose?

72 DPI for web/email, 150 DPI for general use, 300 DPI for printing.

Is it secure?

100% secure. Everything runs in your browser. No files uploaded.
