FAQ

What are the crop units?

Values are in points (1 inch = 72 points). Use the quick presets for common sizes.

Can I crop specific pages?

Yes! Choose All Pages, Current Only, Odd, Even, or Custom page range.

Does cropping remove content?

Cropping sets the visible area (CropBox). Content outside may still exist but won't be visible in viewers.

Is it secure?

100% secure. Everything runs in your browser. No files uploaded.
