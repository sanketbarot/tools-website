Frequently Asked Questions

1

Does cropping reduce file size?

Yes, cropping uses the PDF CropBox which tells viewers to only show the cropped area. The file size may decrease slightly. For maximum size reduction, combine with our Compress PDF tool.

2

Is cropping permanent?

The crop is applied by setting the PDF CropBox and MediaBox. It's the standard method used by Adobe Acrobat. The cropped area will not be visible in any viewer.

3

Can I crop different pages differently?

Currently you can apply the same crop to all pages, the current page only, or a page range. Navigate between pages to preview how the crop looks on each page.

4

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.
