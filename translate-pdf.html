FAQ

Which languages are supported?

50+ languages including English, Hindi, Gujarati, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, Korean, and many more.

How accurate is the translation?

Translation quality is good for general text. Technical/legal documents should be reviewed by a professional translator.

Is there a word limit?

The free API translates in chunks of 500 characters. Large documents are automatically split and reassembled.
