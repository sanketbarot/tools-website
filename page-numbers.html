Frequently Asked Questions

1

Can I change the starting number?

Yes! Set Start Number to any value. You can also add a custom prefix and suffix around each number.

2

Can I skip the first page?

Yes! Use the "Skip First" option to start numbering from page 2. You can also choose to number only odd or even pages, or a specific range.

3

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.

4

What number formats are available?

Plain (1,2,3), Dashes (- 1 -), Parentheses ((1)), "Page X", "Page X of N", and Roman numerals (I, II, III).
