Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDFTools completely free?

Yes! All tools are 100% free to use — no signup, no payment, no hidden fees. We keep the service running through non-intrusive Google Ads.

Are my files safe when using PDFTools?

Absolutely! All processing happens 100% in your browser. Your files never leave your device or get uploaded to any server. Your privacy is fully guaranteed.

A tool is not working. What should I do?

Try refreshing the page and using a modern browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended). Check that your file type is supported. If the issue persists, please contact us using the form above with details about the problem.

Can I request a new PDF tool?

Yes! We love hearing feature requests. Use the contact form above and select "Feature Request" as the subject. We review and consider all suggestions!

Do you offer an API?

Not currently. All tools run client-side in your browser. If you're interested in API access, please contact us with your use case and we'll consider it for the future.

How can I advertise on PDFTools?

For advertising or partnership inquiries, please email us at sanketspprt@gmail.com or use the contact form above with "Advertising" selected as the subject.
