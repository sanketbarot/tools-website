FAQ

Can I use an image as watermark?

Yes! Switch to Image Watermark tab and upload any PNG or JPG. PNG with transparent background works best.

2

Can I tile/repeat the watermark?

Yes! Select Tiled / Repeat pattern to fill the entire page with repeated watermarks.

3

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.
