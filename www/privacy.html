1. Introduction

Welcome to AI ToolCor ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and services (the "Service").

By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe experience for all users.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Files You Process

Good News: We collect absolutely NOTHING from your files. All processing happens locally in your browser.
  • All file processing is done entirely in your browser via client-side JavaScript scripts.
  • Your files are never uploaded to any backend servers.
  • We have zero access to the data or text content of your documents.
  • Files are processed locally and remain strictly inside your device's sandbox memory.
  • When you close the browser tab or refresh, all loaded data is gone permanently.

2.2 Usage Information

We may collect anonymous usage data to improve our service metrics, such as:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system platform
  • Pages visited and time spent on our site

  • General geographic location (country level only)
  • Device type layout (desktop, tablet, mobile)

2.3 Cookies

We use cookies and temporary storage technologies for:

  • Essential cookies: Required for fundamental user interface modules to function properly.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us monitor site traffic and fix system exceptions.
  • Advertising cookies: Used by Google AdSense networks to render relevant programmatic ads.
  • Preference cookies: Save your custom layout settings and tool preferences.

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements across our toolkit pages. This essential integration helps us sustain development and keep all tools free for everyone.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, utilize tracking parameters to serve ads based on your prior browsing behaviors.
Opt Out: You can choose to opt out of personalized advertising pipelines at any time by configuring your Google Ads Settings page or via AboutAds.info.

4. Cookie Consent & Control

By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy framework. You can control or block specific storage items through your local browser preference menus:
