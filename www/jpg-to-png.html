Frequently Asked Questions

1

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes! You can upload multiple JPG/JPEG images and convert them all at once. Download them individually or all together as a convenient ZIP file.

2

Are my images secure and private?

100% secure! All processing happens in your browser. Images never leave your device or get uploaded to any server — complete privacy guaranteed.

3

Will PNG quality be same as JPG?

PNG is a lossless format so your converted image will have the same or better visual quality. PNG also supports transparency which JPG does not.

4

What is the difference between JPG and PNG?

JPG uses lossy compression making files smaller but with slight quality loss. PNG uses lossless compression and supports transparency, making it ideal for logos, graphics and images that need editing.

5

Is there a file size limit?

There is no strict server-side limit since everything runs in your browser. Very large images may take a moment depending on your device speed and available memory.
