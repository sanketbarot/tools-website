Frequently Asked Questions

1

Can I crop all images to the same size?

Yes! Set your crop area on one image, then click "Apply to All". All images will be cropped with the same dimensions. Use Output Width/Height for exact pixel sizes.

2

What is auto-fill crop?

When you apply the same crop to multiple images of different sizes, the tool auto-centers the crop and fills the area. If the image is smaller than the crop, it's centered with white fill.

3

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No! 100% browser-based. Images never leave your device.

4

What formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP are supported. You can also convert the output format (e.g., crop a PNG and save as JPEG).
