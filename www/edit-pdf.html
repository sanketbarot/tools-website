Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about editing PDFs

1

What can I add to my PDF?

You can add text, freehand drawings, highlights, rectangles, circles, lines, and arrows — all with custom colors, sizes and opacity.

2

Can I edit existing text in the PDF?

Yes! With the Select tool active, click on any existing text in the PDF to open an inline editor. Edit the text and click outside to apply the change.

3

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No! Everything runs in your browser using JavaScript. Your PDF never leaves your device — complete privacy guaranteed.

4

Can I edit multiple pages?

Yes! Use the page navigation arrows in the sidebar to switch between pages. Your annotations and text edits on each page are saved separately.

5

Will my edits be permanent in the PDF?

Yes! When you click Save PDF, all your annotations and text edits are permanently embedded into the PDF using pdf-lib. The original content is preserved.
