Frequently Asked Questions

1

Is this a legally valid signature?

This tool adds a visual signature image to the PDF. For legally binding digital signatures with certificates, use a dedicated e-signing service. However, for most informal and business documents, a visual signature is widely accepted.

2

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF and signature never leave your device.

3

Can I sign multiple pages?

Yes! Navigate between pages and place signatures on any page. You can add multiple signatures on different pages.

4

Are my signatures saved?

Signatures are saved in your browser's local storage so you can reuse them. They are never sent to any server.
