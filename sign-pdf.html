FAQ

How do I create a signature?

Three ways: Draw with mouse/finger, type your name with signature fonts, or upload a signature image.

Can I place multiple signatures?

Yes! Click "Add Signature Here" to place multiple signatures on any page.

Is it legally binding?

This creates a visual signature. For legally binding digital signatures, you may need a certificate-based solution.
