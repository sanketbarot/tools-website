Frequently Asked Questions

            
Everything you need to know about font identification

        

        


            

                

                    1
                    
How does Font Identifier work?

                    
                

                

                    

                        
Upload an image with text and our AI analyzes font characteristics like stroke width, serif style, letter spacing and height ratio to find the closest matching fonts.

                    

                

            


            

                

                    2
                    
Is Font Identifier free to use?

                    
                

                

                    

                        
Yes! Font Identifier is completely free. No signup, no registration, no limits.

                    

                

            


            

                

                    3
                    
What image formats are supported?

                    
                

                

                    

                        
We support JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP and BMP image formats. For best results, use a clear, high-resolution image with visible text.

                    

                

            


            

                

                    4
                    
How accurate is the font detection?

                    
                

                

                    

                        
Our AI-powered detection is highly accurate for common and popular fonts. For best results, use clear images with large, well-lit text. Custom or rare fonts may show the closest available match.

                    

                

            


            

                

                    5
                    
Are my images secure and private?

                    
                

                

                    

                        
Your images are only used for font identification and are never stored permanently. Processing is done securely and your data remains private.
