FAQ

How secure are these passwords?

Very secure! Passwords are generated using Math.random() in your browser. They never leave your device. Use 16+ characters with all types for maximum security.

What is entropy?

Entropy measures password randomness in bits. Higher = harder to crack. 60+ bits is good, 80+ is excellent.

What makes a strong password?

Length (16+ chars), mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols. Avoid dictionary words, personal info, and common patterns.
