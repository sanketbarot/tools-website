FAQ

1

How secure are generated passwords?

Very secure! Passwords are generated using cryptographically secure random values (window.crypto). They never leave your browser.

2

What is a strong password?

A strong password has at least 12 characters, mixes uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols, and avoids dictionary words or personal info.

3

Are passwords stored?

No! Passwords are generated locally in your browser and never sent anywhere. We don't store or log any generated passwords.

4

What does crack time mean?

Crack time is an estimate of how long it would take a computer to brute-force guess your password. Longer and more complex passwords take exponentially longer to crack.
