Frequently Asked Questions

1

What is a URL slug?

A URL slug is the part of a URL after the domain name that identifies a specific page. For example, in aitoolcor.com/url-slug-generator, the slug is "url-slug-generator". Good slugs are short, descriptive and SEO-friendly.

2

Why are SEO-friendly slugs important?

Clean slugs improve search engine ranking, are easier to share, and provide better user experience. They tell both users and search engines what the page is about.

3

Should I use hyphens or underscores?

Use hyphens (-)! Google treats hyphens as word separators, while underscores join words together. Hyphens are the SEO standard recommended by Google.

4

Should I remove stop words from slugs?

Yes! Removing common stop words like "the", "a", "an", "and", "is" makes slugs shorter and more focused on important keywords for better SEO.

5

What is the ideal slug length?

Keep slugs between 3-5 words and under 60 characters. Short slugs are easier to remember, share, and rank better in search results.
