FAQ

Does compression reduce quality?

At 75% quality, the difference is barely noticeable. Lower quality = smaller file but more visible compression artifacts.

Which format is best?

JPEG: Best for photos. PNG: Best for graphics with transparency. WEBP: Best balance of size and quality (modern browsers).

Is it secure?

100% secure. All compression happens in your browser. Images never leave your device.
