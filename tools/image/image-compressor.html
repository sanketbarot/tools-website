FAQ

1

How much can images be compressed?

Typically 40-80% size reduction depending on the image and quality setting. JPEG and WebP offer the best compression ratios.

2

Does compression reduce quality?

At 70-80% quality, the difference is barely noticeable to the human eye. Below 50%, quality loss becomes more visible.

3

Are my images uploaded?

No! Everything runs in your browser using Canvas API. Images never leave your device.
