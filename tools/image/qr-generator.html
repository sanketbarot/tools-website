Frequently Asked Questions

1

What is a QR Code?

QR (Quick Response) Code is a 2D barcode that stores data like URLs, text, WiFi credentials etc. It can be scanned by any smartphone camera.

2

Can I customize colors?

Yes! You can change both the QR code color and background color. Make sure there's enough contrast for scanners to read it properly.

3

What formats can I download?

You can download as PNG or JPG. PNG is recommended for sharp quality and transparency support.

4

Is my data stored?

No! The QR code is generated entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
