FAQ

What color formats are supported?

HEX (#ff0000), RGB (255,0,0), HSL (0°,100%,50%), and CMYK (0%,100%,100%,0%). All update in real-time.

What are tints and shades?

Tints = color mixed with white (lighter). Shades = color mixed with black (darker). Useful for creating color schemes.

What is WCAG contrast?

WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) requires minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio between text and background for readability.
