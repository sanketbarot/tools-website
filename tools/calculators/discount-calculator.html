FAQ

How to calculate discount?

Discount = (Discount% ÷ 100) × Original Price. Final = Original - Discount.

What is double discount?

Extra discount is applied on the already discounted price. E.g., 20% + 10% extra on ₹1000 = ₹1000 → ₹800 → ₹720 (not ₹700).

How does tax work with discount?

Tax (GST) is calculated on the discounted price, not the original price.
