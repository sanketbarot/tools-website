FAQ

How to calculate percentage?

Formula: (Part ÷ Whole) × 100. Example: 25 out of 50 = (25÷50)×100 = 50%.

How to calculate percentage increase?

New Value = Original × (1 + Percentage/100). Example: 100 + 20% = 100 × 1.20 = 120.

How to calculate percentage change?

% Change = ((New - Old) ÷ |Old|) × 100. Example: 80 to 100 = ((100-80)÷80)×100 = 25% increase.

What is GST percentage?

India GST rates: 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%. Use our quick buttons to calculate GST on any amount.
