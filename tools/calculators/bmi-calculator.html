FAQ

What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) = weight(kg) ÷ height(m)². It indicates if your weight is healthy for your height.

What is a healthy BMI?

18.5 to 24.9 is considered normal/healthy BMI range.

Is BMI accurate?

BMI is a screening tool. It doesn't account for muscle mass. Consult a doctor for full assessment.
