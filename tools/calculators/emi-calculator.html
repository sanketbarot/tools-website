FAQ

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay your loan. It includes both principal and interest.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1], where P = Principal, R = Monthly rate, N = Number of months.

How to reduce EMI?

You can reduce EMI by: 1) Increasing tenure, 2) Making partial prepayments, 3) Negotiating lower interest rate.
