FAQ

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is dummy placeholder text used in design and typesetting since the 1500s. It helps designers focus on layout without being distracted by readable content.

Where does Lorem Ipsum come from?

It originates from "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (The Extremes of Good and Evil) by Cicero, written in 45 BC. The standard Lorem Ipsum passage has been used since the 1500s.

Why use Lorem Ipsum?

It provides a natural-looking distribution of letters and words, making it ideal for mockups, wireframes, web design, and print layouts where real content isn't available yet.

Can I get HTML formatted output?

Yes! Check the "Include HTML tags" option to get the text wrapped in proper HTML paragraph or list tags, ready to paste into your website code.
