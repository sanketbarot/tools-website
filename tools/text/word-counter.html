FAQ

How does word counting work?

Words are counted by splitting text on whitespace (spaces, tabs, newlines). Numbers and hyphenated words count as single words.

What is reading time?

Reading time is based on average reading speed of 200 words per minute. Speaking time uses 150 wpm.

What is Flesch-Kincaid score?

It measures text readability on a 0-100 scale. Higher = easier. Based on average sentence length and syllable count per word.

Does it work with other languages?

Word and character counting works for all languages. Readability and syllable counting are optimized for English.
