Frequently Asked Questions

1

How are words counted?

Words are counted by splitting text on spaces and line breaks. Consecutive spaces are treated as one separator. Numbers and hyphenated words each count as one word.

2

What is reading time based on?

Reading time is calculated at 200 words per minute (average adult reading speed). Speaking time uses 130 words per minute (average speaking pace).

3

What is the readability score?

The Flesch Reading Ease score ranges from 0-100. Higher scores mean easier text. 60-70 is standard, 80-90 is easy, below 30 is very difficult (academic/legal).

4

What is keyword density?

Keyword density shows how frequently each word appears in your text as a percentage. It helps with SEO optimization — ideally keep primary keywords between 1-3%.

5

Is my text stored?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server. 100% private and secure.
