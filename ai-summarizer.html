FAQ

How does the AI work?

Uses TF-IDF extractive summarization with sentence importance scoring. Runs 100% in your browser — no data sent to servers.

Is it secure?

100% secure. No data sent to any server. Everything runs locally in your browser.

How accurate is the summary?

Very accurate for factual documents. It extracts the most important sentences using statistical analysis (TF-IDF + position scoring).
