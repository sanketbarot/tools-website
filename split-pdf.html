Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract specific pages from a PDF?

Yes! Use "Select Pages" mode to click individual pages, or "Custom Input" to type page numbers like 1,3,5-8,12.

Are my files secure?

100% secure! All processing happens in your browser. Files never leave your device or get uploaded to any server.

Can I split every page into separate files?

Yes! Select "Every Page" mode and each page becomes a separate PDF. Download all as a ZIP file.

Does splitting reduce PDF quality?

No! We extract pages without any modification. All content, images, and formatting stay in original quality.
