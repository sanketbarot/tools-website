Frequently Asked Questions

1

Can I extract specific pages from a PDF?

Yes! Use Select Pages mode to click individual pages, or Custom Input to type page numbers like 1, 3, 5-8, 12.

2

Are my files secure and private?

100% secure! All processing happens in your browser. Files never leave your device or get uploaded to any server â€” complete privacy guaranteed.

Can I split every page into separate files?

Yes! Select Every Page mode and each page becomes a separate PDF. Download all files at once as a convenient ZIP archive.

4

Does splitting reduce PDF quality?

No! We extract pages without any modification. All content, images, fonts and formatting stay in their original quality.

5

How do I use the custom page input?

Type page numbers separated by commas. Use a dash for ranges. For example: 1, 3, 5-8, 12 will extract pages 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 12 into a single PDF.
