FAQ

What types of corruption can be fixed?

Broken cross-references, corrupted page trees, damaged content streams, missing fonts, invalid metadata, and truncated file structures.

Will all content be recovered?

In most cases 90-100% of content is recovered. Severely damaged files may have some data loss on specific pages.

Is it secure?

100% secure. Everything runs in your browser. No files uploaded to any server.
