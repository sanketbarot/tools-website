FAQ

What types of corruption can be fixed?

We can fix broken cross-references, corrupted page trees, damaged content streams, missing fonts, and invalid metadata.

Will all content be recovered?

In most cases, we can recover 90-100% of the content. Severely damaged files may have some data loss.
