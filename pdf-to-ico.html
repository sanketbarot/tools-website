Frequently Asked Questions

1

Which page of the PDF is converted?

The tool always converts the first page of the PDF into the favicon icon. This is ideal for PDF covers, logos, or title pages.

2

Should I download all sizes?

Yes! The "All Sizes (ZIP Pack)" option is best if you want to use the icon on websites, apps, and multiple devices. It includes 16×16, 32×32, 48×48, 64×64 and 128×128.

3

What background should I choose?

White background works best for most PDF documents. Use Transparent if your PDF has a logo on a light background that you want to blend with browser themes.

4

Is my PDF safe and private?

Yes! Everything happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your PDF file is never uploaded to any server — complete privacy guaranteed.

5

What is the difference between Contain and Cover?

Contain fits the entire PDF page inside the icon without cropping. Cover fills the icon area completely, which may crop parts of the page edges.
