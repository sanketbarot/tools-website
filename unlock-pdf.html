Frequently Asked Questions

1

Do I need the original password to unlock?

Yes, you need the correct password that was set on the PDF. This tool removes protection once you provide the right password. We cannot crack or bypass unknown passwords.

2

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF and password never leave your device.

3

What types of protection can be removed?

We remove open passwords and permission restrictions (printing, copying, editing). After unlocking, you can freely use the PDF.

4

Will unlocking change my PDF content?

The content is rendered at high resolution and embedded as images. Text may not be selectable, but all visual content is 100% preserved.
