Frequently Asked Questions

1

Do I need the original password to unlock?

Yes, you need the correct password that was set on the PDF. This tool removes protection once you provide the right password. We cannot crack or bypass unknown passwords.

2

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No! Everything runs in your browser using JavaScript. Your PDF file and password never leave your device — complete privacy guaranteed.

3

What types of protection can be removed?

We remove open passwords (required to view the PDF) and permission restrictions (printing, copying, editing). After unlocking, you can freely use the PDF.

4

What if my PDF has restrictions but no password?

If your PDF only has permission restrictions without an open password, we can remove those restrictions automatically — no password needed! The tool detects this.

5

Will unlocking change my PDF content?

No! Only the password protection is removed. All content, images, fonts, and formatting remain 100% intact without any quality loss.
