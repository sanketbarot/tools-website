Frequently Asked Questions

1

What can I add to my PDF?

You can add text, freehand drawings, highlights, rectangles, circles, lines, and arrows. All with custom colors and sizes.

2

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No! Everything runs in your browser using JavaScript. Your PDF never leaves your device — complete privacy guaranteed.

3

Can I edit multiple pages?

Yes! Use the page navigation arrows in the sidebar to switch between pages. Your edits on each page are saved separately.

4

Will my edits be permanent in the PDF?

Yes! When you click Save PDF, all your annotations are permanently embedded into the PDF using pdf-lib. The original content is preserved.

5

Can I undo my edits?

Yes! Click the Undo button in the sidebar to remove the last annotation. You can also Clear Page to remove all edits from the current page.
