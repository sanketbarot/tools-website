FAQ

Will formatting be preserved?

Yes! We preserve headings, bold, italic, tables, lists and paragraph formatting from your Word document.

What formats are supported?

Both .doc (Word 97-2003) and .docx (Word 2007+) are supported.

Is it secure?

100% secure. Everything runs in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server.
