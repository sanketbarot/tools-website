Frequently Asked Questions

1

Is formatting preserved?

Yes! Bold, italic, underline, font sizes, colors, headings, lists, tables, and images are all preserved in the PDF output.

2

Is my document uploaded to any server?

No! 100% browser-based. Your document never leaves your device.

3

What file formats are supported?

This tool supports .docx files (Office 2007+). Old .doc format is not supported. Save as .docx in Word first.

4

Are images included in the PDF?

Yes! Embedded images (JPEG, PNG) from the Word document are extracted and included in the PDF output.
