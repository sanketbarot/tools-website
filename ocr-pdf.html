FAQ

What is OCR?

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) converts images of text into actual editable text. It "reads" characters from scanned documents.

Which languages are supported?

100+ languages including English, Hindi, Gujarati, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, and many more via Tesseract.js.

How accurate is it?

95-99% accuracy on clear documents. Quality depends on image resolution, font clarity, and language.

Is it secure?

100% secure. Tesseract.js runs entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
