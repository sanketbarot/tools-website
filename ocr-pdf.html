FAQ

What is OCR?

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) is technology that converts images of text into actual editable and searchable text. It reads characters from scanned documents.

Which languages are supported?

We support 50+ languages including English, Hindi, Gujarati, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, and many more.

How accurate is the OCR?

Our AI-powered OCR achieves 95-99% accuracy on clear documents. Accuracy depends on image quality, font, and language.
