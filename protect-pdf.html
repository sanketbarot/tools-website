Frequently Asked Questions

1

What type of encryption is used?

We use RC4-128 bit encryption (PDF Standard Security Handler Revision 3), compatible with Adobe Acrobat, Chrome, Firefox, and all major PDF readers.

2

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF and password never leave your device.

3

What if I forget my password?

We do not store your password. If you forget it, the PDF cannot be recovered. Always save your password safely!

4

Will protection affect PDF quality?

No! Adding a password only adds encryption. All content remains 100% intact.
