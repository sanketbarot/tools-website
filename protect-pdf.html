FAQ

How secure is the protection?

We add a password requirement to open the PDF. The PDF metadata includes encryption markers. For maximum security, use AES-256 encryption.

Can I set permissions?

Yes! You can allow or deny printing, copying, editing, annotations, form filling, and extraction independently.

Is it secure?

100% secure. Your password and PDF never leave your browser. All processing is done locally.
