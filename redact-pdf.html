Frequently Asked Questions

1

Are redactions truly permanent?

Yes! Each page is flattened to an image with black rectangles burned in. No hidden text, metadata, or layers remain. The original content is completely destroyed.

2

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.

3

Can I redact specific text automatically?

Yes! Use the Search & Redact feature to find all occurrences of names, phone numbers, emails, etc. across all pages.

4

Will the output PDF be selectable text?

No — to ensure redactions are permanent, each page is saved as an image inside the PDF. Text selection will not be available.
