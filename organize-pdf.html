FAQ

1

Can I reorder pages by dragging?

Yes! Simply click and drag any page thumbnail to a new position. The page order updates instantly.

2

Can I delete specific pages?

Yes! Select pages using checkboxes, then click "Delete Selected". Or click the trash icon on individual page cards.

3

Can I duplicate pages?

Yes! Select pages and click "Duplicate". The copies are inserted right after the original. Duplicated pages are marked with a yellow badge.

4

Is there undo support?

Yes! Click "Undo" or press Ctrl+Z to reverse the last action (reorder, delete, duplicate, etc.).

5

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.
