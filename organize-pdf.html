Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rearrange pages by dragging?

Yes! Simply click and drag any page thumbnail to a new position. The page order updates instantly.

Can I delete specific pages?

Yes! Hover over any page and click the red × button to remove it. You can also select multiple pages and delete them at once.

Can I add pages from another PDF?

Yes! Click "Add PDF" in the toolbar to insert pages from another PDF document.
