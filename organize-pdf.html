FAQ

Can I reorder pages by dragging?

Yes! Just click and drag any page thumbnail to a new position. The order updates instantly.

Can I delete specific pages?

Yes! Hover over a page and click the red × button, or select multiple pages and click Delete in the toolbar.

Can I duplicate a page?

Yes! Hover and click the blue copy button, or select pages and click Duplicate in the toolbar.

Is there an undo feature?

Yes! Click the Undo button in the toolbar to reverse your last action.
