Frequently Asked Questions

1

How does table detection work?

The tool analyzes text positions in the PDF to detect rows and columns. Items at similar Y-positions form a row; items at similar X-positions form a column. This works well for text-based PDFs with structured tables.

2

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No! 100% browser-based. Your PDF never leaves your device. All processing happens locally.

3

What if my PDF has multiple tables?

Each detected table becomes a separate worksheet in the Excel file. The sheet is named by page number and table index.

4

Can I convert scanned PDFs?

Scanned PDFs are image-based and contain no selectable text, so text extraction is not possible without OCR. This tool works best with text-based PDFs.

5

Can I open the XLSX in Google Sheets?

Yes! The output is a standard .xlsx file fully compatible with Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice Calc, and Numbers.
