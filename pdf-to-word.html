Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PDF to Word conversion

1

How accurate is the PDF to Word conversion?

For text-based PDFs, text extraction is highly accurate with preserved headings and structure. For scanned/image PDFs, text content may be limited since browser-based OCR is not applied.

2

Is my PDF uploaded to any server?

No! 100% browser-based. Your PDF never leaves your device — complete privacy guaranteed.

3

Can I edit the converted Word file?

Yes! The output is a standard .docx file fully compatible with Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and LibreOffice.

4

Are images from the PDF included?

Currently text content is extracted and placed in the Word document. Image extraction is not supported in browser-based conversion.

5

Which layout option should I choose?

Preserve Layout keeps each line as-is — best for structured documents like invoices or reports. Flowing Text merges lines into proper paragraphs — great for articles, books, or essays.
