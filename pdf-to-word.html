FAQ

Will formatting be preserved?

Text is extracted with paragraphs and page breaks. The DOCX output is formatted and editable in Word. Complex layouts may be simplified.

Can I convert scanned PDFs?

This tool works with text-based PDFs. For scanned/image PDFs, use our OCR tool first to extract the text.

Is it secure?

100% secure. Everything runs in your browser using JavaScript. No files are uploaded to any server.

What output formats are available?

You can download as .DOCX (Word), .TXT (plain text), or .HTML (web page). DOCX preserves formatting best.
