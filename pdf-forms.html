FAQ

Can I fill existing PDF forms?

Yes! Upload any PDF with form fields and we'll detect and let you fill all text fields, checkboxes, and dropdowns.

Can I create new forms?

Yes! Use the "Create New Form" tab to add text fields, checkboxes, dropdowns, dates, and more.
