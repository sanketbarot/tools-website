FAQ

1

What's the difference between Fill and Create?

Fill: Upload a PDF, click on it to type text, place checkboxes, dates. Create: Build a form from scratch — add labels, fields, checkboxes, signature lines, then generate a new PDF.

2

Can I create multi-page forms?

Yes! When creating forms, if fields exceed one page, new pages are added automatically.

3

Is my data private?

100% private. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
