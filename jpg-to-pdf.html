FAQ

1

Why is the quality better than other tools?

Most tools re-compress images (JPEG→Canvas→JPEG = quality loss). This tool uses pdf-lib to embed original file bytes directly — your JPEG data goes into the PDF untouched. Zero quality loss!

2

What about PNG images?

PNG files are embedded using pdf-lib's embedPng() which preserves the full PNG data including transparency. No conversion to JPEG needed!

3

What is "Fit to Image" page size?

Each PDF page is exactly the size of the image (based on DPI). No margins, no scaling, no quality loss. Perfect for archiving photos.

4

Is my data private?

100% private. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
