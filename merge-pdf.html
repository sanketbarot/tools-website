Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free to merge PDFs?

Yes! 100% free. No signup, no watermarks, no limits. Everything runs in your browser.

Are my files secure?

Yes! Your files never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. No server uploads!

How many PDFs can I merge?

There's no hard limit! You can merge as many PDFs as your browser memory allows. We've tested with 50+ files successfully.

Does merging reduce quality?

No! We combine PDFs without any compression or quality loss. All pages remain in their original quality.
