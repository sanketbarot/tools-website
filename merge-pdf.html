Frequently Asked Questions

1

Is it really free to merge PDFs?

Yes! 100% free. No signup, no watermarks, no limits. Everything runs in your browser — no server uploads ever needed.

2

Are my files secure and private?

Yes! Your files never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server — complete privacy guaranteed.

3

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

There's no hard limit! You can merge as many PDFs as your browser memory allows. We've tested with 50+ files successfully.

4

Does merging reduce PDF quality?

No! We combine PDFs without any compression or quality loss. All pages, images, fonts and formatting remain in their original quality.

5

Can I reorder files before merging?

Yes! Simply drag and drop the files in the list to change their order. On desktop you can also use the up/down arrow buttons next to each file.
