Frequently Asked Questions

1

What types of differences are detected?

The tool detects added text (green), removed text (red), and changed text (yellow) at the word level across all pages.

2

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No! Everything runs in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

3

Can I compare scanned PDFs?

Text comparison works only with text-based PDFs. The Visual mode shows visual differences for scanned PDFs using pixel comparison.

4

What view modes are available?

Side by Side: Both pages shown simultaneously. Overlay: One on top of the other with opacity. Slider: Drag to reveal A or B with a divider.
