FAQ

What is PDF/A?

PDF/A is an ISO-standardized version of PDF designed for long-term archival. It embeds all fonts, removes external dependencies, and ensures the document will be readable decades from now.

Which PDF/A version should I choose?

PDF/A-1b is the most widely accepted and compatible. Use PDF/A-2b for documents with transparency or JPEG2000 images. PDF/A-3b allows embedded attachments.

Is PDF/A required for legal documents?

Many government agencies, courts, and regulatory bodies require PDF/A format for official document submissions and long-term record keeping.
