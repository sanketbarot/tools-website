FAQ

What is PDF/A?

PDF/A is an ISO-standardized version of PDF designed for long-term archival. It embeds all fonts, removes external dependencies, and ensures the document will be readable decades from now.

Which version should I choose?

PDF/A-1b is most widely accepted and compatible. Use PDF/A-2b for transparency/JPEG2000. PDF/A-3b allows embedded file attachments.

Is PDF/A required for legal documents?

Many government agencies, courts, and regulatory bodies require PDF/A format for official submissions and long-term record keeping.
