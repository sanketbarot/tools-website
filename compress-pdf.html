Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PDF compression

1

Does compression reduce PDF quality?

Our compression renders each page and re-encodes images at optimized quality. With Low compression, quality difference is barely visible. Maximum compression may show slight quality reduction on very detailed images — ideal for email/web sharing.

2

How much can file size be reduced?

Typically 20–70% reduction depending on the PDF content and chosen level. PDFs with lots of metadata, duplicate objects and unoptimized structure compress the most.

3

Are my files secure and private?

100% secure! All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server — complete privacy guaranteed.

4

Can I compress password-protected PDFs?

We attempt to process protected PDFs, but some heavily encrypted files may not be compressible without the password. Try removing the password first using our Unlock PDF tool.

5

Which compression level should I choose?

Medium is recommended for most use cases — great balance between file size reduction and quality. Use Low for print-quality documents and Maximum for emails or web sharing.
