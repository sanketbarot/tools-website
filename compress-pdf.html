Frequently Asked Questions

Does compression reduce PDF quality?

Our compression removes unnecessary metadata, optimizes the PDF structure, and reduces redundant data. With low and medium compression, quality loss is minimal to none.

How much can file size be reduced?

Typically 20-70% reduction depending on the content and compression level. PDFs with lots of metadata, duplicate objects, and unoptimized structure compress the most.

Are my files secure?

100% secure! All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files never get uploaded to any server.

Can I compress password-protected PDFs?

We attempt to process protected PDFs, but some heavily encrypted files may not be compressible without the password.
